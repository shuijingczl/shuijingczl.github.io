## Biography

I am a M.S. student in Precision Instrument and Machinery at the School of Automation, [Nanjing University of Science and Technology (NJUST)](https://www.njust.edu.cn/). Previously, I received my B.Eng. in Electrical Engineering and Automation from Shanghai University of Electric Power (SUEP) in 2026.

My current research focuses on high-precision MEMS gyroscopes, specifically exploring analog interface circuits, FPGA-based closed-loop control, and error compensation algorithms.

---

## Research Interests

- **MEMS Inertial Sensors**: High-precision MEMS gyroscopes and capacitive interface circuit design.
- **Control & Embedded Systems**: FPGA-based digital closed-loop control, real-time operating systems (RTOS), and microcontroller applications.
- **Signal Processing & Algorithms**: Sensor drift compensation, fault diagnosis, and machine learning applications in instrumentation.
